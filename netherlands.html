Support in the Netherlands

IMA Afscheidszorg

Professional bereavement care organisation providing compassionate farewell services for babies who are stillborn or die in the neonatal period. Available across the Netherlands.

Dutch specialist centre for parents after baby loss. Provides multilingual information cards, support resources, and guidance in Dutch, English, Arabic, Turkish, Polish, and more.
