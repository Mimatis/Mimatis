Journal & remembrance

Grief does not end. It changes.

Losing a baby is not something that happens and then stops. Parents carry their baby with them for the rest of their lives. That is why we share art, music, books and things made by hand: as a way to cope, a way to remember, and sometimes a way to escape for a while when you need to.

Makers

Made with care for families

People who create for families after baby loss. We feature their work because of the care that goes into it.

Handmade caskets & urns · Netherlands

Hart van Hout

In 2007 Arnaldo Lopez lost his son Silvan at 29 weeks. Unable to find something that felt right, he made a casket himself, shaped as a heart of wood. Since 2010 he has made heart-shaped caskets, urns and keepsakes by hand for babies at every stage of pregnancy.

Meet other parents

Make something together, with people who understand

Spending time with parents who have lived the same loss, and doing something together with your hands, can mean a great deal. If you offer a workshop, a walk, a craft or a remembrance activity for bereaved parents in your country, let us know, and we will help families near you find it.
