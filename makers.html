Makers

Made with care for families

Some of the most meaningful things after the loss of a baby are made by hand, often by people who have lost a baby themselves: a soft basket to carry your baby, an urn that feels right, a keepsake for a brother or sister.

These are makers we have come to know. We feature them because of the care that goes into their work.

Baskets, caskets and urns

For carrying, holding and laying your baby to rest.

Carrying baskets, small caskets & memory stars · Netherlands

Sterrenkindje

After her son Jonas was stillborn in 2011, Janine van Veen began making soft carrying baskets, small caskets and memory stars, so that parents can keep their baby close and care for them.

One memory star is laid with the baby and one stays with the parents. On the day of the funeral they swap them, so that afterwards parents have a star that smells of their baby.

Urns for babies & children

My Little Bear

After her son Sieb was stillborn at 39 weeks, Carlijn found very few urns that felt right to her. She started My Little Bear to make urns for babies and children, shaped like a heart, a star, a cloud or a sun.

Handmade caskets & urns · Netherlands

Hart van Hout

In 2007 Arnaldo Lopez lost his son Silvan at 29 weeks. Unable to find something that felt right, he made a casket himself, shaped as a heart of wood. Since 2010 he has made heart-shaped caskets, urns and keepsakes by hand for babies at every stage of pregnancy.

Art and keepsakes

Something to keep at home, for parents, brothers and sisters.

Lettering & illustration

Viktorija Liau

Hand-lettered names, illustrations, prints and stickers in bold, joyful colour. A baby’s name on canvas gives it a place at home; a sticker that says Sister gives an older child something of their own.

Before you order

Delivery can take time, especially to another country. If you need something in the first days, ask the maker about delivery before you order, and ask your hospital or funeral director what is possible where you live.

How we choose makers

MIMATIS is a non-profit association. Being listed here is free: we do not take payment from makers. We tell their stories because of the care in their work, and we ask them to let us know if anything changes.
