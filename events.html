Coming soon · MIMATIS Events

A day to say your baby’s name out loud again.

Grief after losing a baby does not end when you leave the hospital. It changes, and it stays with us for years. That is why MIMATIS thinks about parents not only in the first days, but in all the years that follow.

We believe that real-life events matter. They are where you meet others who understand, hear stories like your own, make a new memory, or simply have a day when your baby is remembered with you.

Hospitals, associations and families already hold many of these events, but they are hard to find. We are building one place to find them all.

What we are looking for

Events for families after the loss of a baby

From hospitals, associations, parent groups, faith communities, artists and makers, in any country and any language. Free or paid, small or large, for the first weeks or many years later.

  • Remembrance servicesCandle evenings, yearly memorials, 15 October
  • Support groupsFor mothers, fathers, couples or grandparents
  • Crafts and creative workshopsMemory boxes, writing, art, music
  • Talks and conferencesFor parents, professionals, or both
  • Faith and ceremonyServices from any faith community
  • Walks and gatheringsMemorial walks, planting days, picnics
  • Online eventsOpen to parents wherever they live
  • For brothers and sistersEvents for children and young people
