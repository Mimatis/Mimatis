Memorial garden

A place for your baby’s name

The MIMATIS memorial garden is a quiet online garden for babies who died during pregnancy, at birth or soon after. Plant a flower in your baby’s name. It blooms alongside flowers planted by parents around the world, and it stays there, always.

Plant a flowerRead about seed letters

For every baby, at any time

After a miscarriage, a stillbirth, the death of a newborn or a termination for medical reasons, many parents look for somewhere to say their baby’s name. Some parents have no grave to visit, and few people ever met their baby. The garden is one small place where your baby is remembered.

You can plant a flower in the first days after your loss, or many years later. There is no wrong time.

How to plant a flower

  1. Write your baby’s nameThe name you gave, or a name you choose now.
  2. Choose a flowerThere are eight to choose from.
  3. Add the year, if you wishThen plant. Your flower appears in the garden.

The flowers: Cherry blossom, tulip, daisy, hibiscus, bouquet, sunflower, rose and lotus.

We keep only your baby’s name, the flower and, if you add it, the year. No account, no email and no other personal details. The flowers and names can be seen by everyone who visits the garden.

Go to the garden and plant a flower

A flower you can plant at home

MIMATIS seed letters are made of paper with poppy seeds inside. Write to your baby, plant the letter in a pot or a garden, and in time the words become flowers that can return every year.

It is a gentle activity for brothers and sisters too: they can write, or draw what they feel, and plant it together with you.

Read more in Seed letters and Flowers that remember. Would you like seed letters for your family or your hospital? Write to contact@mimatis.org.

15 October, a day to remember together

15 October is Pregnancy and Infant Loss Remembrance Day. Many families light a candle at 7 pm. Read how countries mark the day in 15 October: how Europe remembers, or find a remembrance event near you on our events page.

Questions about the garden

Can I plant a flower after a miscarriage?

Yes. The garden is for every baby who died during pregnancy, at birth or soon after: after a miscarriage, a stillbirth, the death of a newborn, or a termination for medical reasons.

Can I plant a flower for a baby who died many years ago?

Yes. It is never too late to remember your baby. Adding the year is optional.

Does it cost anything, and do I need an account?

No. Planting a flower is free, and you do not need an account or an email address.

What do you keep, and who can see it?

We keep only your baby’s name, the flower you choose and, if you add it, the year. The flowers and names can be seen by everyone who visits the garden.

What are seed letters?

MIMATIS seed letters are made of paper with poppy seeds inside. You write to your baby, plant the letter in a pot or a garden, and in time the words become flowers.
